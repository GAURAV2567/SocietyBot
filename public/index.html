FAQ

Got questions?

Do residents need to download any app?
No. Everything runs on WhatsApp — which residents already have. No new apps, no logins, no confusion. Adoption is instant, even for elderly residents.
How does the Visitor Management System work exactly?
Guard logs visitor via WhatsApp (name, purpose, vehicle). Resident gets an instant WhatsApp notification and taps Allow or Deny. Entry is logged with timestamp and visible in the admin dashboard.
What payment gateway do residents use?
Razorpay — supporting UPI, Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets. On Starter and above, you connect your own Razorpay account so payments land directly in your society's account.
Can I switch from SaaS to Perpetual License later?
Yes. If you're on a SaaS plan and decide to purchase a Perpetual License, we'll credit up to 3 months of paid SaaS fees toward the license price.
How long does setup take?
Approximately 30 minutes from booking to a live WhatsApp bot for your society. We handle the entire technical setup — you just share your society name and Razorpay details.
Can guards who aren't tech-savvy use the system?
Yes — guards interact via simple WhatsApp messages. They send visitor details in a simple format and the bot handles the rest. Guard training takes under 10 minutes.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — send "Hi" to +91 81470 97058 on WhatsApp to try the live demo instantly. Paid plans come with a 2-month free trial period with no credit card required.
Can the Agency License be resold to clients?
Absolutely. The Agency License includes unlimited deployments, white-label rights, and full resell rights. You keep 100% of what you charge your clients.
