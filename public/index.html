FAQ

Got Questions?

Do residents need to download any app?
No. Everything runs on WhatsApp — which residents already have. No new apps, no logins, no confusion. Adoption is instant, even for elderly residents.
What payment gateway do residents use?
Razorpay — supporting UPI, Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets. On Starter and above, you connect your own Razorpay account so payments go directly to your society's account.
Can I switch from SaaS to Perpetual License later?
Yes. If you're on a SaaS plan and decide to purchase a Perpetual License, we'll credit up to 3 months of paid SaaS fees toward the license price.
How long does setup take?
Approximately 30 minutes from booking to a live WhatsApp bot. We provide a guided onboarding session for all paid plans and handle the technical setup end-to-end.
What happens after the update period on Perpetual?
Your bot keeps working — you just won't receive new features or patches automatically. You can subscribe to our Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) for ₹4,999–9,999/yr to stay updated, or self-maintain the code.
Can the Agency License be used for my own society?
Absolutely. The Agency License covers unlimited deployments — whether for your own projects, client deployments, or both. White-label and resell rights are included at no extra cost.
What data does the admin dashboard show?
The admin dashboard (Pro and above) shows all registered users, payment history, complaint status and resolution tracking — with the ability to update statuses and auto-notify residents via WhatsApp.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — scan the QR code or send "Hi" to +91 81470 97058 on WhatsApp to try the live demo instantly. Paid plans come with a 2-month free trial period.
